> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · October 31, 2023

In games, players are familiar with the concept of “respawning.” Think back to Super Mario, getting another shot after tumbling into a pit, or to a fallen character in *Genshin Impact* being revived to continue the quest. Respawning is not only about failure. It is a pause, a breather, a chance to reconsider and return with renewed energy.

Real-life challenges—especially the pressure of professional life in bustling Manila—can sometimes feel as though we have run out of lives. Inspired by those virtual adventures, what if we treated the moments when our energy runs low as opportunities to respawn?

Allowing ourselves time to recharge is not surrender, but a strategic play. In *Genshin Impact*, you would not send a character with low health into a major battle. Relentlessly pushing through fatigue can cloud judgment, sap energy, and hinder creativity.

## Respawn points in and around Manila

**Local cafés.** Find a familiar corner where the city outside can recede for a while. A favorite drink and a little distance can feel like a well-timed health potion.

**Escapes to nature.** Manila’s parks may be sparse, but a drive to Tagaytay or Baguio offers cooler air and greener surroundings: real-world sanctuaries with the calm of a safe area in a game.

**The art scenes in BGC.** Art can interrupt routine. BGC’s murals and galleries offer an invitation to step out of the everyday for a moment and let the mind wander.

When burnout casts its shadow, remember the value of a checkpoint. Take a moment, find a place that lets you pause, and return with more of yourself available for whatever comes next.
