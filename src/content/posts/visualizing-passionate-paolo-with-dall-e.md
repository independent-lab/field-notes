> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · October 20, 2023

Once upon a time in the bustling heart of Metro Manila, there lived a young and creative digital marketer named Paolo. Passionate Paolo, as he was known to his friends, had a vision: to bring his vivid imagination to life through artificial intelligence. His trusty sidekick, a stuffed bear, was always by his side, ready to join him on adventures in the digital realm.

Paolo’s days were filled with the challenges of his profession, the demands of a digital world, and the elusive idea of work-life balance. He wanted to find harmony between his career and personal interests while sharing a sense of mindfulness, growth, and play.

Then Paolo encountered DALL·E, a tool that could generate images from written descriptions. It offered a way to visualize himself and his bear in different scenes using prompts alone.

The results turned those ideas into a set of black-and-white illustrated moments: commuting through Manila, working, pausing, and moving through the city with the bear close at hand.

In 2023, that translation from a few lines of text into a coherent recurring character still felt novel. The images were imperfect, but they made the possibility tangible. Paolo and his bear could move between the real and imagined versions of the city, with AI acting as a new kind of sketchbook.
