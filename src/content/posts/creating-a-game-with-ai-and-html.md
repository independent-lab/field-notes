## Diwata versus Kapre

*Diwata vs. Kapre* began with the idea of an age-old rivalry. Diwata, a graceful wind mage, draws on the power of the breeze. Kapre, a steadfast guardian of the earth, embodies stone and terrain. The meeting of air and land became the heart of a simple shooting game.

What set the project apart for me was how it was made. I used DALL-E to create its visual elements, from the thick jungle backdrop to the central characters. By working from prompts and combining the resulting images with simple HTML, I was able to build something playable despite having no coding experience.

One prompt—“Pixel art chibi character set in a captivating Filipino-inspired magical fantasy RPG world known as Diwata”—led to Diwata’s design. A similar direction produced Kapre, and another created the background.

The original desktop game used the up and down arrow keys to move and the spacebar to shoot. The goal was to stop enemies from getting past the player.

With *Diwata vs. Kapre*, I wanted to see what could happen when tradition met a new technology: Filipino folklore reimagined through AI-assisted art and a first encounter with HTML.

## 2026 preservation note

The original character art, background, and game assets survive in the Siklab Serye media archive. The original playable code has not yet been confidently recovered, so the game is not being presented as restored.
