> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · November 11, 2023

Tucked inside Unimart Estancia, The Coffee Shop offered a comfortable stop for familiar Filipino food. On this visit, the dish that stood out was the lechon kawali.

## The plate

Lechon kawali depends on contrast: crisp, golden skin around tender pork. This serving delivered that contrast, with a crackle in each bite and enough softness underneath to keep the dish from feeling dry.

The dipping sauce added acidity against the richness. With steamed rice and a drink, it made for a straightforward, satisfying meal.

The restaurant’s relaxed setting suited the dish. It felt like a place for an unhurried lunch or dinner rather than an occasion that needed planning.

At the time, the plate cost ₱220. What stayed with me was how well a familiar dish had been handled: crisp where it needed to be, tender beneath, and served without unnecessary complication.
