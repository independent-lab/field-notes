> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · October 14, 2023

Life with bulldogs is full of love, surprises, and lessons in paying attention. Bacon and Beer, our two 72-pound companions, recently went in for a routine vet visit. What began with vaccines also revealed a problem we had not seen.

## A day full of sniffs and surprises

The two arrived at the clinic with their noses working overtime, investigating every interesting scent while the vet handled their routine vaccinations.

Beneath their usual energy, however, something quieter was happening. The vet noticed changes in their coats and identified a fungal infection affecting both dogs. Until a professional caught it, the problem had been easy to miss under all that bulldog enthusiasm.

## The unseen problem

That visit reminded us that care is not limited to the obvious. Changes in a coat, an unfamiliar bald spot, or signs of discomfort can be small clues. In Bacon and Beer’s case, the fungal problem had remained hidden from us even as they carried on with their normal appetite for play and mischief.

With guidance from their vet, they began treatment and the slow trip back to healthier coats.

## Beyond the vaccination checklist

The day changed how I thought about a routine appointment. Vaccines mattered, but the visit also gave a trained pair of eyes the chance to notice something we had missed.

Bacon and Beer’s story is not a universal guide to canine health, and another dog’s symptoms may mean something different. For us, it was a lesson in observation: preventive care is partly about creating opportunities to catch the quiet problems before they grow louder.
