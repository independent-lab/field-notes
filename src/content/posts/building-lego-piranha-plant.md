> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · November 11, 2023

Building the LEGO Super Mario Piranha Plant was never only about assembling bricks. As a longtime Mario fan, it felt like bringing a piece of the Mushroom Kingdom onto my shelf.

## From pipe to plant

The build begins with the familiar green warp pipe. From there, the stem rises toward the spotted head, hinged jaw, and wonderfully exaggerated teeth.

Each stage brought back afternoons spent in front of the television, controller in hand, trying to pass the Piranha Plants that emerged at exactly the wrong moment. Here, the old obstacle changed character. Instead of avoiding it, I could slow down and understand how its shape came together.

## A display piece with a memory inside

The finished model is playful and immediately recognizable. Its movable head, mouth, leaves, and stem give it more personality than a static ornament. On the shelf, it has become a conversation starter—an invitation to trade stories about Mario games and the different moments when they entered our lives.

That is what I enjoyed most about the set. It connected two kinds of play: the digital rhythm of running and jumping through Mario’s worlds, and the physical satisfaction of making something one brick at a time.

The completed Piranha Plant is a modern display piece, but the memory inside it is much older.
