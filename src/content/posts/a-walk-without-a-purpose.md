The efficient walk edits the world. It selects a destination, calculates a route, and turns everything between here and there into background. This is useful when the destination matters. It is limiting when the point is to notice where you already are.

## Let the street make the plan

A purposeless walk follows weaker signals: a patch of shade, the sound from a side street, a building seen from an unfamiliar angle. The body makes small decisions before the mind can turn them into a program.

The reward is not novelty in the dramatic sense. It is the discovery that familiar places have been changing without asking for our attention. A shop closes. A tree grows over a sign. Someone places a chair beside a doorway at the same hour each afternoon.

To walk without a purpose is to practice being present without demanding that presence become productive.
