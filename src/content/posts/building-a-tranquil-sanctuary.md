> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · October 19, 2023

I bought a steel rack for around 1,000 pesos because I needed useful storage. What I did not expect was how calming the work of assembling it would become.

## Taking shape

I laid the pieces out on the floor and began putting them together slowly. The process was straightforward, but it held my attention. With each part secured, the rack became less like a collection of metal and boards and more like a place waiting to be used.

There was a meditative quality to the repetition. For a while, the next fitting and the next shelf were the only things that needed solving.

## Making room for personality

Once it was standing, the useful part of the project was complete. The more personal part began when I filled the shelves with books, plants, and objects that reflected my interests.

It was still an inexpensive rack. But the things it gathered—and the small act of making space for them—changed the corner around it.

The project reminded me that calm does not always require getting away from ordinary life. Sometimes it comes from working with your hands, placing one piece after another, and giving the things you care about somewhere to belong.
