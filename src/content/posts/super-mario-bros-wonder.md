> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · November 11, 2023

As a long-time fan of the Super Mario franchise, I approached *Super Mario Bros. Wonder* with a mix of excitement and nostalgia. From the beginning, the game did not disappoint. It blended classic Mario charm with playful twists that gave the series fresh energy.

The graphics were a delightful surprise. They retained the colorful, whimsical character of earlier games while adding a modern polish that made every level feel vibrant. Each world brought its own visual identity and challenges.

The gameplay remained true to the Super Mario spirit: intuitive, engaging, and just challenging enough. The controls felt familiar but refined, while the new power-ups and abilities encouraged different ways to move through each level.

## Familiar rules, new surprises

The level design was the standout. Each stage balanced nostalgia for long-time fans with discovery for new players. Inventive obstacles and hidden details rewarded exploration and experimentation.

The badge system added another layer. Its movement and ability options made familiar stages feel more open to personal strategy without losing the directness that makes a Mario game easy to pick up.

The soundtrack deserved special mention, too. It paid homage to familiar tunes while introducing compositions that fit the game’s sense of adventure and discovery.

Multiplayer was where *Super Mario Bros. Wonder* truly shone for me. Playing with friends added a welcome layer of joy and chaos, making it an easy choice for a gathering or family game night.

The game respected what made the series endure while still finding room to surprise. Whether playing alone or with friends, it felt both comfortably familiar and genuinely new.

**Rating: 9/10**
