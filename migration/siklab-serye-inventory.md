## Archive treatment recommendation

Use a consistent notice immediately below the article header:

> **From the archive**  
> Originally published on Siklab Serye · October 23, 2023

Retain the original publication date in metadata and archive chronology. Do not use the migration date as `pubDate`.

For a piece with a substantial new note, add a second, clearly separate line:

> Updated for Field Notes · [Month D, 2026]

Use the update line only when new editorial material is actually added. Preserve the original body as a historical statement, then label the new material **2026 update** (or similarly explicit wording). Silent modernization would weaken the archive's value.
